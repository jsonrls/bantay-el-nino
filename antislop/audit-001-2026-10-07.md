## Findings & Resolutions

| # | Rule | Tier | Priority | Finding & Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **R-29** (Palette Cap) | Purpose-Gate | **MEDIUM** | In `src/components/browser-alert-card.tsx` and `src/components/community-reports-card.tsx`, raw Tailwind `emerald` classes were used for active banners. **RESOLVED:** Replaced with the semantic `risk-low` status token (`#4D7C0F` / `bg-risk-low/10 text-risk-low border-risk-low/30`), maintaining the strict 2 core + 1 accent + semantic status palette cap. |
| 2 | **R-30** (Contrast & Legibility) | Quality Lock | **LOW** | Subtext in active alert state was `text-[11px]`. **RESOLVED:** Boosted to `text-xs` for clear outdoor sunlight legibility. |
| 3 | **R-03** (Mobile Layout Reflow) | Hard Gate | **HIGH** | Inputs in `community-reports-card.tsx` and buttons in `browser-alert-card.tsx` were previously `h-8` (32px), failing the 44px minimum thumb tap target. **RESOLVED:** Updated all interactive form inputs and primary buttons to `min-h-[44px]` on mobile devices with `w-full sm:w-auto` responsive wrapping. |
| 4 | **R-04** (Icon Relevance & No Emojis) | Purpose-Gate | **MEDIUM** | `live-weather-card.tsx` previously used `📍` emoji in badge labels. **RESOLVED:** Replaced with semantic Lucide `<MapPin />` icon matching `DESIGN.md` icon policy. |
| 5 | **R-03** (Bottom Navigation Clearance) | Hard Gate | **HIGH** | Bottom mobile nav bar on devices with home indicator bars (iOS) lacked safe-area padding. **RESOLVED:** Added `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]` to mobile nav and expanded `<main>` bottom padding to `pb-20 md:pb-0`. |
